Find where a model update made predictions worse, responses slower, or calls more expensive. Compare releases to narrow down who was affected, then build evaluation datasets from the production examples that exposed the regression.

## Connect your app

- [Add an SDK](https://docs.wildedge.dev/setup/sdk) to record model calls in Python, Android, or Swift apps.
- [Connect your LLM gateway](https://docs.wildedge.dev/llm-gateways) to track latency, token usage, and cost without changing your app.

If your app runs models locally and calls an LLM gateway, [set up both together](https://docs.wildedge.dev/setup/sdk-and-gateway).
